# Keepwork AI Pet language

Vocabulary for the AI Pet planning work and its relationship to existing Keepwork concepts.

## Language

**AI Pet**:
A companion interface to Keepwork AIChat for conversation and useful assistance, with personality and light pet interactions.
_Avoid_: Virtual pet game, independent assistant identity

**Pet appearance**:
The character artwork and visual presentation of a pet. Changing appearance does not by itself mean changing the conversation partner.
_Avoid_: Persona, account

**Desktop pet**:
The floating companion surface of the desktop application through which a user interacts with the current AIChat conversation or persona.
_Avoid_: Separate conversation, standalone pet service

**Mobile pet app**:
A dedicated pet-focused application for AIChat conversation and assistance that works without a paired desktop.
_Avoid_: Desktop remote control, desktop-dependent companion

**Persona**:
A configured AIChat agent identity with a role and its associated interactions. A persona is distinct from the artwork through which it is presented.
_Avoid_: Skin, pet appearance

**Official persona catalog**:
The collection of official AIChat characters available for eligible users to choose as companions. It is distinct from the collection of a user's private configured agents.
_Avoid_: All users' personas, appearance catalog

**Official character**:
A companion identity offered through the official persona catalog, with its designated character and matching appearance. Choosing it does not expose another user's conversations.
_Avoid_: Private custom agent, skin

**Conversation transcript**:
The recorded messages exchanged in a conversation. A transcript is distinct from a summary or a user-confirmed long-term memory.
_Avoid_: Memory

**Cross-device continuity**:
The ability to use the same selected persona, pet appearance, and conversation history on phone and desktop. It does not by itself imply sharing microphone sessions or synchronizing confirmed long-term memories.
_Avoid_: Identical platform features, live call handoff

**Voice session**:
A user-started conversation containing multiple spoken turns, which can continue while another app is visible or the screen is locked. The user can speak over the pet to interrupt its reply.
_Avoid_: Single recording, permanent wake-word listening

**Spoken interruption**:
The user speaking during the pet's reply to stop that reply and take the next turn. This does not by itself end the voice session.
_Avoid_: End call, mute

**Home service region**:
The service region associated with an account, retained when the user travels. It is distinct from the user's current location or selected language.
_Avoid_: Current country, interface language

**Voice allowance**:
The amount of voice usage available to an account through its free trial or purchased entitlement.
_Avoid_: Unlimited voice, model token budget

**History migration**:
The transfer of eligible existing desktop conversations into the same account's shared conversation history. It is distinct from moving an account's history between service regions.
_Avoid_: Region migration, persona publication

**Eligible consumer history**:
Existing completed consumer conversations with verified account ownership, an official-character mapping, and a supported source format. Private custom-agent work, operational exchanges, unfinished drafts, and unmapped history are outside this set.
_Avoid_: All local history, every user/assistant message
