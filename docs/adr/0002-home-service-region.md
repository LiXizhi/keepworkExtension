# Stable home service region

An account retains its home service region when its user travels; travel does not automatically migrate conversation history. The user chose this over globally shared history or separate regional accounts, keeping account continuity while making any future region migration an explicit operation with its own design.

This decision establishes service ownership semantics, not physical data-residency guarantees or a legal conclusion. How the home region is assigned, which deployments serve it, cross-country service availability, and any future export/migration mechanism remain to be specified and verified.
